Protocol

Webcam eye tracking, measured

How closely does it
actually follow your eyes?

WebGazer infers gaze from a webcam with no special hardware. This bench calibrates it, then measures the error against known targets and reports it in degrees of visual angle — the same unit a research eye tracker is specified in.

Needs webcam permission. Nothing leaves this machine — inference runs in the page.

Phase 01

Set the camera up
the way you will use it

  1. Sit as you normally would and set the distance above to match. Every angle in the report scales off it.
  2. Light your face from the front. A window behind you turns your face into a silhouette and tracking collapses.
  3. Fill the feedback box in the preview, then keep your head still. WebGazer has no head-pose compensation — a 2 cm shift after calibration shows up as error.

Calibration teaches the model your eyes. It has to happen before any prediction is meaningful.
