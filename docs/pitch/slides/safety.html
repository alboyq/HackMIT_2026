Safety by design

It must never rush your face.

Slows as your face gets closer

Backs off if it presses into anything

On a fault it holds. It never drops.

Arriving too fast at the head is ranked the worst outcome; dropping the food is second. Zero unsafe arrivals at the head in 160 simulated runs. Closing speed at the face stayed under half the limit we set. Touching the patient ends the episode with the largest penalty.
